# Changelog

All changes to Jumps, newest first. The addon updates itself through the CurseForge app;
after an update, type `/reload` in game (or restart the game when the notes say so).

## [1.8.14] - Unreleased

- **Combo:** keep jumping and your combo grows - every jump within 2 seconds of the last one adds to it. It shows on the on-screen counter while it's running and changes colour the longer it gets. Your longest combo is on the Overview tab, and a new best is announced to you in chat.
- **New "Combo" column on the leaderboard:** everyone's longest combo. It shows for players on this version or newer. The window is a little wider to fit it.
- **More prestige stars to earn:** your star used to stop changing fairly early. Now it keeps getting a new look, further and further apart, long into the prestige grind. Nobody's current star changes. **Restart the game once** after this update to see the new stars (a `/reload` isn't enough for new images).
- Two challenges had the same name; one of them has a new name now. Nothing else changes - if you'd done it, it stays done.

## [1.8.13] - 2026-11-12

- **Fair duels everywhere:** in a duel, jumps now count no faster than normal jumping on flat ground - it no longer matters where you stand. (Some spots let you land early and score far more.) Outside duels nothing changes: every jump still counts, also up hills and stairs.
- **No more draws when it's level:** a duel that ends on the same score goes to whoever got there first. (Both need this version; otherwise it's a draw as before.)
- **"Best duel" starts over** for everyone, so the scores can be compared again. It shows for players on this version or newer.
- Dueling someone on an older version still works; a score above what the new rule allows is cut down to it on your screen.
- **Two challenges got reachable goals:** since only full jumps count, they asked for more jumps than you can fit in the time. Your best so far counts, so if you've already beaten the new goal, it completes when you log in.

## [1.8.12] - 2026-11-11

- **Guildmates and friends you'd been in a party with no longer drop off the board** when you update. The 1.8.11 clean-up sometimes took them for strangers from a group (they came back the next time they jumped). It now checks your guild and friends lists first.

## [1.8.11] - 2026-11-11

- **A new, shorter name: Jumps** - the same as the `/jumps` command, and a lot shorter at the start of every chat line. Nothing else changes: your progress, settings and commands stay. (The download is now called `Jumps-1.8.11.zip`; the folder inside is still `JumpCounter`.)
- **Your own leaderboard:** players you meet in a random dungeon, raid or battleground no longer show up on your leaderboard, duel you or appear in your chat. Your board is your guild, your friends and the people they know first-hand - every guild and group of friends has its own. Strangers already on your board are cleared once, and friends on older versions show up again as soon as they update.
- **Guilds and friends on the leaderboard:** new buttons above the leaderboard show everyone, only your guild or only your friends.
- **Guild ranking:** a new view that ranks the guilds themselves, with each guild's jumps added up. Hover a guild to see its jumpers.
- **`/jumps forget <name>`** stops listing one of your characters (one you've deleted, say) - for friends too. Its jumps still count in your total, and logging in on it lists it again.
- Guild names show for players on this version or newer.

## [1.8.10] - 2026-11-10

- **A weekly goal for everyone together:** this week's jumps of everybody on your leaderboard are added up against three goals. Each one you reach together gives XP to everyone who did at least 500 jumps that week. It's at the top of the Challenges tab. (This first week it counts from when you get the update.)
- **More challenges:** there are now 100 to find.
- **Skipped jumps fixed:** a jump pressed at the exact moment you land was sometimes not counted. It is now.
- **Fairer counting:** pressing jump while you can't actually jump - lying dead, netted, stunned - no longer counts.
- **A tidier leaderboard:** players who've been gone for two weeks drop off it (and come back when they do).
- The "new version" message in chat now shows for each new version, not just the first one that day.

## [1.8.9] - 2026-11-10

- **The duel window is small again:** who you're up against, the score and the clock - now in the same look as the on-screen counter. The big window with portraits and hopping figures is gone.
- **New "Best duel" column on the leaderboard:** everyone's most jumps in a single duel, next to their wins. It shows for players on this version or newer, and counts duels since 1.8.7.
- The "This week" column is gone from the leaderboard - your own week is on the Overview tab and the counter, and everyone's is in their hover tooltip.

## [1.8.8] - 2026-11-10

- **Your session survives a /reload:** "this session" now carries on after a `/reload` or a restart of the game, as long as you're back within 10 minutes - so updating the addon no longer costs you a long session. (It starts counting this way from this version on.)
- **The Prestige button moved** to the top of the window, next to your jump level, and shows on every tab once you can prestige.

## [1.8.7] - 2026-11-10

- **22 new challenges** to find, in eleven new kinds. Most of them start counting from this update.
- **Fairer duels:** hopping on the spot against a tree or a wall no longer counts faster than normal jumping.
- **New duel window:** you against your opponent - portraits, big scores, a bar showing who's ahead, and a little figure for each of you that hops along.
- **New Overview tab:** your big numbers, your next rank and the challenges you're closest to, your jumps over the last 7 days, and your records.
- **The on-screen counter** got the same look as the rest of the addon.
- The leaderboard now opens sorted by this hour (until you pick a column yourself).

## [1.8.6] - 2026-11-10

- **Prestige rebalanced:** stars now come at jump level 100, and challenges give half the XP (also the ones you've already done). Everyone's stars are recalculated once from their total XP. Jumps, duels, crowns and daily challenges keep all their XP.
- **Your prestige star changes look** as you collect more stars - with a special message when it does.
- **A fresh look for the window:** your name, rank, jump level and XP bar are at the top of every tab, the top three on the leaderboard get medals, and the leaderboard tells you how far it is to the player above you.
- **New Challenges tab:** an icon for every kind of challenge, clearer progress bars, and a bar showing how many you've completed.
- **New on-screen counter:** your total, jump level, XP bar and place on the leaderboard.
- **Friends' level-up messages now say the class** ("...is now a level 42 Warrior!"), so they aren't mistaken for jump levels.
- The prestige challenges were lowered to match the new pace.

## [1.8.5] - 2026-11-09

- **Jumps at the water surface are counted properly**, even when you keep hopping.
- **Jumps while running uphill** no longer get skipped.
- **Jumping underwater doesn't count** - with or without water breathing.
- **No jump duels while swimming.**
- **New: friends' character level-ups in chat** - at level 10 and 20, then every level from 30. Turn it off in Settings.
- Cleaner prestige messages.

## [1.8.4] - 2026-11-09

- **Duels are now 30 seconds** - short and intense.
- **Duels go on when you die:** keep jumping as a ghost - dying doesn't stop or cancel a duel, and you can be challenged while dead.
- **/jumps duel tells you exactly why** someone can't duel right now, and automatically tries someone else.
- Players who just dueled aren't picked again while they're still on cooldown.
- Fixed friends sometimes showing as "via friends" even though you're friends directly.

## [1.8.3] - 2026-11-08

- **Prestige rebalanced:** jump levels go faster, so a new prestige star comes every few days of play - still at jump level 50. Everyone's stars are recalculated once from their total XP, so the same XP always means the same stars. You'll see a message with your new count.
- **Duel ranks are now rated:** beat someone and you win points from them - more if they're ranked higher. New duelists don't lose points, and after a few losses in a row you get a free one.
- **Live duel scores:** your opponent's score updates about every second during a duel.
- Crown messages now always catch up with whoever really holds the crown.
- Level-up messages are a bit quieter.

## [1.8.2] - 2026-11-08

- **A brand new look** that fits right into World of Warcraft, with tabs along the bottom: Overview, Leaderboard, Challenges and Settings.
- **The leaderboard is a table** - click any column to sort by it, and see everyone's title at a glance.
- **Daily challenges!** Three new ones every day for extra XP. Everyone gets the same ones - race your friends.
- **Duel ranks!** Every duel earns duel points and moves you up the ranks. Even a loss counts.
- Duels happen more often when more of you are jumping - but never the same two players more than every 30 minutes.
- Prestige now unlocks at jump level 50. Stars you already earned are kept.
- "Avg/hour" now compares everyone fairly over the last 7 days.
- Fewer crown messages at the start of each hour.
- Smoother messaging between players - duel results no longer get lost when many people are online.

## [1.8.1] - 2026-11-08

- The crown race stays much closer to live for everyone - including friends of friends and the other faction.
- Fixed friends sometimes not seeing each other's latest jumps (in-game friends with two-part names, and Battle.net showing a friend as offline).
- New players now see everyone online right away.
- Random duels only pick players who are actually jumping.
- Smoother: less work in the background with big friend lists.
- The new-version notice is now a short chat line instead of a popup.
- Hovering a player shows when and how their score last arrived.

## [1.8.0] - 2026-11-08

- **The crown!** A fresh race every hour - who can claim it? Check the new tab on the leaderboard.
- Jump duels are shorter and snappier.
- Fixed an error popup during boss fights.
- A few new things to discover in the Challenges tab.

## [1.7.3] - 2026-11-08

- The addon now goes by **JumpingCounter**, same as on CurseForge. Nothing else changes - your progress stays.
- The "new version" window now points straight to CurseForge and explains how to turn on automatic updates.
- Your full jump duel record is now shared with friends, not just your wins (both of you need 1.7.3).

## [1.7.2] - 2026-11-08

- **JumpCounter is on CurseForge!** The "new version" window now shows the download links, ready to copy. `/jumps update` opens it any time.
- Random jump duels no longer start while you're on a flight path.

## [1.7.1] - 2026-11-08

- **Fixed an error popup when jumping in combat.** Your jumps were still counted - the error came from a challenge check that the game no longer allows during combat.
- One challenge that couldn't work under the game's combat rules has a new goal.

## [1.7.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Jump duels are now 2 minutes.** Both players need v1.7.0 or newer to duel.
- **Fair jumping:** only full jumps count now. Spamming tiny hops (for example when stuck against a wall) no longer counts faster than real jumping.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Friends of friends:** players now pass on the scores they know, so you'll see more people on the leaderboard - including the other faction through a Battle.net friend. Those rows say "via friends" when you hover them.
- **Faction icons** next to every name (once that player is on v1.6.0).

## [1.5.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Jump duels!** Now and then you'll be paired with another player who's jumping around for a 3-minute jump-off. Most jumps wins - and everyone online will hear about it. Can't wait? Type `/jumps duel`. Duels can be turned off in Settings.
- Both players need v1.5.0 or newer to duel.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Update notices:** from now on, when a newer version is out you get a popup at login with the download link ready to copy, and the window shows "Update available". `/jumps update` shows the link any time.

## [1.3.1] - 2026-11-08

- **Fixed duplicate players on the leaderboard.** Old copies of players saved by earlier versions (first name only, or an outdated second row of the same player) are cleaned up automatically when you log in.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-08

- **Account-wide:** your jumps, level, prestige and challenges are now shared by all your characters. One leaderboard row per account; hover it to see each character.
- **Minimap button:** click to open, right-click for settings, drag to move. Hide it in Settings or with `/jumps minimap`.
- **Lots of new ranks** to earn - and they go higher than ever.
- **Many more challenges** to discover.
- Characters sharing a first name are kept apart, and each shows its full name. `/jumps name` sets it by hand if needed.
