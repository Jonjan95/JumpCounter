# Jumps

**Count every jump - and find out who in your guild really jumps the most.**

Jumps turns your jumping in World of Warcraft into a game of its own: a leaderboard with your guild and friends, jump levels and prestige stars, over 100 challenges, quick jump duels, an hourly crown race and a weekly goal you work on together.

It works by itself from the moment you install it. Everyone in your guild or on your friends list who also has the addon shows up on your leaderboard automatically - no setup, no accounts. Every guild and group of friends gets its own leaderboard: a stranger you meet in a dungeon or battleground never ends up on yours.

## What you get

- **A jump counter** for all your characters together, on screen if you want it: your total, jump level, XP bar and place on the leaderboard.
- **A leaderboard** with your guild, your friends, your Battle.net friends (also on the other faction) and the people they know. Sort it by total jumps, this hour, average per hour, duel rank and more. Show everyone, only your guild or only your friends.
- **A guild ranking:** the jumps of each guild on your leaderboard added up.
- **Jump levels and prestige:** every jump is XP. Reach jump level 100 and you can prestige for a star - and the star changes look as you collect more.
- **Jump ranks:** your all-time jumps earn you titles. Only your next one is shown; the rest are yours to discover.
- **Over 100 challenges** to find, from the obvious to the odd, plus **three daily challenges** that are the same for everyone - race your friends.
- **A weekly goal for everyone together:** your whole leaderboard's jumps that week count towards three shared goals.
- **Jump duels:** now and then you're paired with another player who is jumping around for a 30-second jump-off. Most jumps wins. Duels are rated, so there are duel ranks to climb. Can't wait? `/jumps duel`.
- **The crown:** whoever jumps the most this hour holds it. A new race every hour.
- **Your own numbers:** jumps per day over the last week, records, deaths, and more on the Overview tab.

## How to use it

Type **`/jumps`**, or click the Jumps button on the minimap. Everything is in one window with four tabs: Overview, Leaderboard, Challenges and Settings.

`/jumps help` lists all commands. The Settings tab lets you choose which chat messages you get, and turn duels and the on-screen counter on or off.

## Fair play

Only real jumps count. Hopping on the spot against a wall doesn't count faster than normal jumping, pressing jump while you can't actually jump (dead, stunned, underwater) doesn't count at all, and each player's jumps are counted by their own addon.

## What is shared with other players

Only your jump numbers: your character's name, class, level, faction and guild name, your jump totals and progress in the addon, and the names and jump counts of your other characters (hide one with `/jumps forget`). It goes only to other players who have the addon, through the game's own addon channels - your guild, your friends and Battle.net friends, and the people they know first-hand. Players you only meet in a group never get it. Nothing is sent anywhere outside the game.

## Install

Install **Jumps** with the CurseForge app (game: World of Warcraft, the Forever client) and turn on automatic updates. After an update, type `/reload` in game.

By hand: download the latest file from this page, unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` so you get `AddOns\JumpCounter\JumpCounter.toc`, and restart the game.

## Good to know

- Your jumps, level, prestige and challenges are shared by all your characters on the same computer.
- Jumps from before you installed the addon aren't known - everyone starts counting from their first day with it.
- Players you haven't heard from in two weeks drop off your leaderboard, and come back when they do.
- Some features need both players on a recent version. The addon tells you in chat when a new version is out.
